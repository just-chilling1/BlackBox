# Academy Series Style — Mandatory Structure & Recurring Blocks

Owner-approved voice for ALL academy scripts (Track B), extracted from the owner's
hand-edited academy set bundled in `assets/voice-reference/` (`academy-first-core-tool.md`,
`academy-premium-with-ad.md`, `academy-final-no-ad.md`). Those files are the canonical
reference — match their register, not the dashboard persuasion scripts.

Applies on top of `HUMAN-TUTORIAL-STYLE.md`. Where they conflict, THIS file wins for academy videos.

## 1. The series is an arc, not standalone videos

Academy videos are one continuous training the member watches in order. Encode that:

- **First academy video (core tool):**
  `[excited] Hey, welcome to the first video of your in-depth training! In this video, we will tackle [Tool Name] and how to make the most out of it.`
- **Every later video:**
  `[excited] Hey, welcome back! In this video, we will tackle [Tool Name] and how to make the most out of it.`
- **Premium features are numbered in viewing order:**
  "your first premium feature ... Accelerator", "your second premium feature : Recurring Stream", "the third premium feature : Social Payouts". Count restarts at the first upgrade video.
- **Final academy video opener:**
  `[excited] Hey, welcome back to the final video of your training In this video, we will tackle [Tool Name]...`
- **Sign-offs:** every academy video ends with `I'll see you in the next one!` — EXCEPT the final video, which ends with `And that was all for the training .. I'll see you inside`.

## 2. Recurring blocks (use these near-verbatim, adapted to the product)

**Prerequisite callback** — every academy video after the first assumes the core tool was used.
Right inside the "[calm] Quick picture..." paragraph:

> You already launched at least one offer in the [Core Tool] — if you didn't .. go ahead and watch the previous video(s), you'll get a step by step guide on how to do it ... so let's continue.

Adapt the prerequisite to what the video actually needs (an offer, a saved link, etc.).

**Support close** — whenever support is mentioned (usually in the closing paragraph, plus at
any failure point):

> use the contact form on your Dashboard or Support in the sidebar. My team usually responds within 2 hours, but allow us 24 to 48 hours on busy days.

**"Here is how:" click path** — when telling the member to do something on ANOTHER page,
give the exact click path in one breath:

> Here is how: click Offers Library on the left-hand sidebar, find your offer card, click on it, scroll to [section], and [action].

**Premium navigation, both ways** — premium/upgrade videos give both routes to the page:

> Open [Tool], you can find it either on the left hand side on the dashboard under the support form , or in the menu on the left under Premium Features.

(Mine the real dashboard layout — quick links may sit elsewhere per product.)

**Free-training ad block** — see §4 for WHEN. The wording when it fires:

> See that gold banner [under the progress / that comes up]? If you still see it — go ahead and click it now. That's free training we run on scaling this system from a few hundred dollars to one thousand or even five thousand dollars a day. Registration's free for our members, but access is limited, and it won't be up forever. Click [real button label], register in the new tab, and come back. [Your thread/clone/posts] will be waiting. [short pause] Okay — back to [the tool].

## 3. Voice — how the owner actually writes

- **Merged, flowing paragraphs.** Transitions run inline at the END of the previous thought:
  "...That's what this tool writes for you. So let's start... Open [Tool]..." — do NOT
  start a fresh paragraph for every step. Audio tags may sit mid-paragraph at the shift
  (`...then hit Generate posts. [warmly] It runs for about a minute...`).
- **Standard transitions:** "So let's start...", "Then we move to...", "Quick picture of...",
  "[short pause] Okay — back to...".
- **Casual punctuation is intentional:** ".." and "..." as breath pauses, spaces before
  punctuation ("support form ,"), lowercase after dashes and in casual runs ("first one is
  your hook", "post 2 through 9"), "wanna". Keep it. Do NOT polish it into clean prose.
- **Clean spelling, loose grammar.** Words must be spelled correctly so TTS pronounces them
  ("that's", "didn't", "wherever") — but keep the casual grammar and punctuation around them.
- **Personality beats, 1–3 per script:** a chuckle line (`That's the whole tool ... [chuckles] easy right .`),
  an exclamation of delight ("and that's it!", "all of that with just one click"), a
  real-world flex ("AES-256 encryption thats how banks secure their system!" → spell "that's").
- **One emotional-stakes line per script** tying the tool to the member's life:
  "one link to change their life and also yours!", "the one that will change your life!",
  "isn't about making money — it's about saving it!".
- **Self-aware video reference** where the page embeds the training video:
  "the training video — that's this one you are watching right now!".

## 4. Free-training ad placement (hard rule)

The ad ONLY belongs in videos where a **real on-screen generation/build wait** happens
(site build, thread generation, clone progress, bulk post run). It is woven into that wait moment.

- **No wait state → no ad.** Library videos, security/account videos, and any walkthrough
  without a loading state get ZERO free-training mentions. Do not force it in.
- Dashboard videos (Track A) keep their own mapped CTA (e.g. gold card on the Dashboard) per `00-setup.md`.
- Never narrate the production rule ("one mention per video...") — same ban as always.

## 5. Closing formula (academy)

1. `[excited]` action paragraph: ONE concrete task with a deadline ("tonight", "within
   twenty-four hours", "THIS week"). "Your action:" prefix optional — the owner drops it sometimes.
2. Support close (block above) if there's a plausible failure mode.
3. Sign-off per §1.
